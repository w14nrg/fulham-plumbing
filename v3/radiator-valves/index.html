Radiator valves

We replace leaking and seized radiator valves, TRVs and lockshields, and take radiators off and refit them for decorating. A pair of valves usually takes about an hour (£75 for the first hour, then charged in 30-minute steps).

Common signs

  • A valve weeps or shows green or rust staining
  • A TRV is stuck fully on or off
  • A valve will not turn
  • A radiator stays cold while others warm normally

A valve-level fault is usually straightforward to separate from a wider heating-system problem. We work on the radiator and valves, not the heat source itself.

Before we arrive

If water is leaking heavily from a valve, protect the floor and avoid repeatedly forcing the spindle. Let the radiator cool before touching the fitting.

Common causes

Gland packing wear

A small weep from around the spindle often comes from worn packing or a seal.

Stuck TRV pin

A thermostatic valve pin can stick after a long period in one position, leaving the radiator permanently off or on.

Corroded valve body

Older valves can corrode around threads and unions, making replacement safer than repeated tightening.

Air or deposits

A cold radiator is not always a valve fault; air or internal deposits can also affect circulation.

What we check and do

Check the fault

We confirm whether the problem is at the TRV, lockshield, union or radiator itself.

Isolate or drain as needed

The circuit is made safe with the least disruption that the existing valves allow.

Replace the valve

Suitable new valves are fitted and unions resealed.

Refill, bleed and test

The radiator is bled, pressure is restored on sealed systems and every disturbed joint is checked.

If the fault lies in the wider heating system, we explain that a heating engineer is needed. Removing and refitting a radiator for decorating is within this service.

In Fulham homes

Local context matters.

Decorating older houses often exposes valves that have not moved for years. Changing a poor valve while the radiator is already off can avoid another visit.

Questions

Common questions

Can you change a valve without draining the system?

Sometimes local isolation reduces the amount that must be drained, but the safe method depends on the existing valves and system.

Can you take a radiator off for decorating?

Yes. We can isolate, remove and later refit the radiator, checking the valves and unions when it goes back on.

Why is my radiator cold at the top?

Air is a common cause, while a cold bottom can suggest deposits. A radiator that is cold all over may also have a closed or stuck valve.

Can you fix my heating?

We handle radiator and valve work. Faults in the wider heating system or heat source are for a heating engineer.
