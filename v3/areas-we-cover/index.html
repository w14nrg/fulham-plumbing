Local detail

Fulham neighbourhood notes.

Munster VillageThe grid of terraced streets around Munster Road sits between Fulham Palace Road and Fulham Road. Many houses have been converted into flats or extended upwards, so shared supplies and long vertical pipe runs are common features to understand.

Top-floor showers can expose weak tank-fed flow, while older pipework and stopcocks may have been altered several times as the house changed. A clear route from the incoming supply helps when several flats share part of the original system.

Jobs we often do here: Shower pumps · Low water pressure · Stopcock replacement

Peterborough EstateSouth of New King's Road, the red-brick late-Victorian terraces are largely family houses. Extended kitchens, loft work and decades of plumbing alterations can leave several generations of valves and pipework in one property.

Loft tanks, vented cylinders and pumped upstairs showers are all relevant here. The most useful visit often starts by understanding which stored-water equipment still serves which outlets before any part is replaced.

Jobs we often do here: Cold water tanks · Hot water cylinders · Shower pumps

Eel Brook Common & Walham GreenBetween Parsons Green and Fulham Broadway, the housing mixes terraces, mansion blocks and flats above shops. That makes leaks between properties and worn fittings in tenanted homes common small-job calls.

The practical challenge is often access and isolation rather than the visible fault itself. A running toilet or leaking tap may be simple once the correct valve and responsible flat have been identified.

Jobs we often do here: Leak repairs · Toilet repairs · Landlords and agents

Bishop's Park & Fulham PalaceThe streets between Fulham Palace Road and the river contain period terraces and mansion flats close to Putney Bridge. Household plumbing here ranges from ordinary tap and valve repairs to garden-water points and radiator work.

Match days at Craven Cottage can change access and parking near the river, so visits need realistic timing. Inside the homes, older fittings often benefit from better local isolation when they are repaired.

Jobs we often do here: Tap repairs and replacement · Radiator valves · Outside taps

Crabtree & Fulham ReachThe riverside streets around Crabtree Lane sit beside modern Fulham Reach apartments. Older terraces and recent riverside flats therefore appear side by side, with very different water systems.

Shower valves, appliance connections and leaks in apartments need a different approach from the stored-water arrangements found in some older homes. The postcode is deliberately not printed for this section.

Jobs we often do here: Shower repairs · Leak repairs · Washing machine and dishwasher plumbing
