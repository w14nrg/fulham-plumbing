Tap repairs and replacement

Dripping, stiff, loose or leaking around the base?

Dripping or stiff taps can often be repaired with a new washer or cartridge, and a new tap usually takes about an hour to fit: £75 for the first hour, then charged in 30-minute steps. We will fit a tap you have bought, as long as it suits your water pressure.

Common signs

  • A drip from the spout when fully off
  • Water around the base or handle
  • A tap that is hard to turn
  • Whistling or banging during use
  • Weak flow from one tap

Weak flow from a single fitting may be the tap, its isolation valve, an aerator or a pressure mismatch rather than a whole-house problem.

Before we arrive

If water is escaping, isolate the nearest safe valve or the main stopcock. Keep water away from electrical fittings and do not open powered equipment.

Common causes

Washers and cartridges

Traditional taps use washers while many mixers use ceramic cartridges. Both wear, and hard London water can shorten the life of moving parts.

O-rings and seals

Water around a handle or base often comes from a local seal rather than the spout washer.

Loose fixings

A tap that moves on the sink can stress flexible tails and seals until a leak begins.

Pressure mismatch

Some modern taps need more pressure than a tank-fed hot supply can provide, making a new tap disappointingly slow.

What we check and do

Identify the tap

We establish whether it uses a washer, cartridge or manufacturer-specific insert and whether parts are serviceable.

Isolate locally

Where possible the tap isolation valves are used so the rest of the home can remain on.

Repair or replace

The failed washer, cartridge, O-ring or complete tap is changed and poor flexible tails are renewed where needed.

Check flow and leaks

Hot and cold are run, the tap fixing is tightened and every disturbed connection is checked.

Customer-supplied taps are welcome, but they need to suit the available hot and cold pressure. The minimum-pressure figure should be checked before buying.

In Fulham homes

Local context matters.

Older houses can have tank-fed hot water. A high-pressure mixer bought online may then give weak hot flow even when the cold side looks normal.

Questions

Common questions

Is a dripping tap worth repairing?

Usually, if the tap body is sound and the correct internal part is available. A good tap can last much longer with a new washer, cartridge or seal.

Can I supply my own tap?

Yes. Send the model or a link first if possible so its pressure requirement and connection sizes can be checked.

Why do taps wear out faster in London?

The local water is hard, so limescale builds on cartridges, aerators and moving surfaces. Many taps can still be serviced rather than replaced.

Why is my new tap so slow?

A tap designed for higher pressure than the hot supply provides is a common cause. A partly closed valve, blocked aerator or restricted pipe can look similar.
