Questions

What people ask first.

How much does a plumber cost in Fulham?

£75 for the first hour, with further time charged in 30-minute steps, with parts at cost. There is no separate call-out fee.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Small repairs are the core of the service: taps, toilets, valves, wastes, pumps and lists of smaller jobs.

Can you come today?

Availability varies. Contact us with the problem and postcode and we will tell you the earliest realistic time.

Do you bring parts?

Send a clear photo if you can. It helps identify the fitting and improves the chance of bringing the right parts on the first visit.

Which areas do you cover?

Fulham SW6 is the core area, with selected nearby work in Chelsea Harbour and Lots Road, Putney and Wandsworth Town.

Do you work for landlords and agents?

Yes. Access can be arranged with tenants, with photos and clear invoicing for the landlord or agent.

What we don't do

We don't work on boilers or gas appliances, we don't do drain jetting, and we don't fit new bathrooms. For a complete new bathroom or en-suite, see En-Suites & Bathrooms.

Need help?

Send us a photo of the problem.

A clear photo and your postcode are often enough for us to tell you what the first visit is likely to involve.
