Shower repairs

Most shower faults come down to a worn cartridge, limescale or a supply problem, and many are fixed within an hour or two: £75 for the first hour, then charged in 30-minute steps. We repair mixer and thermostatic showers in Fulham SW6.

Signs you need this

  • Drips after the shower is turned off
  • Temperature swings during use
  • Weak spray from the head
  • A stiff, loose or spinning handle
  • Water leaking from the valve body
  • A bath/shower diverter that will not switch

Replacing the whole valve before checking the cartridge and supplies can waste money, so the symptom and supply arrangement come first.

Before we arrive

Do not dismantle a powered shower or touch wet electrical controls. For mixer showers, isolate the water if a valve is leaking continuously.

Common causes

Worn cartridges

Thermostatic and flow cartridges contain seals and moving parts that eventually stiffen, leak or stop regulating temperature correctly.

Limescale

Scale can restrict a shower head, hose, filter or cartridge and cause weak flow or unstable temperature.

Unbalanced supplies

A mains-fed cold and tank-fed hot supply can have very different pressures, and the valve must suit that arrangement.

Failed diverters

Bath/shower mixers and exposed valves use diverters and seals that can stick or leak after years of use.

What we do

Identify the valve

We look for the make, model and cartridge type before ordering or removing parts.

Check both supplies

Hot and cold flow are compared so a supply fault is not mistaken for a bad valve.

Service the shower

Cartridges, seals, strainers, hoses and heads are cleaned or replaced as required.

Test temperature and shut-off

The repaired shower is run through its range and checked for drips after it is turned off.

Exposed valves can often be replaced like for like. If a concealed valve can only be reached by disturbing fixed finishes, we explain that first.

What it usually costs

Questions

Why does my shower run hot and cold?

A worn thermostatic cartridge, blocked strainers or unequal hot and cold supplies can all cause swings. Checking both supplies avoids replacing the wrong part.

Can you get parts for an old shower?

Often, if the make and model can be identified. A clear photo helps. Very old or obscure valves sometimes have no sensible parts route.

Repair or replace the valve?

If the body is sound and the cartridge is available, repair is normally the first choice. Replacement becomes sensible when parts are obsolete or the body is damaged.

Do you fit electric showers?

We can assess the plumbing connection, but electrical work required for a powered shower is for a suitably qualified electrician.
