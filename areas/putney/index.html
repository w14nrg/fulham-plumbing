Plumber in Putney, SW15

Putney is just over Putney Bridge from our base, and we cover it mainly north of the Upper Richmond Road. Expect the same clear pricing and small-jobs service as in Fulham.

The homes here

Putney mixes Victorian and Edwardian terraces with mansion blocks, 1930s purpose-built flats and larger houses nearer the river. Those building types produce a wide range of water-supply arrangements within a relatively compact area.

Period houses may still use loft storage and pumps for upstairs showers. Purpose-built blocks can have longer shared supply routes, while larger houses often have several floors and more isolation points to identify.

What goes wrong here

Tank-fed showers that feel weak, ageing loft tanks and older cylinders are common reasons to inspect a period property. Mansion flats can also have worn toilets and taps where fittings have been changed many times without the underlying isolation being improved.

Buyers often want pressure, visible pipe materials and storage equipment checked before exchange. That makes a focused plumbing inspection useful alongside the wider purchase process.

Jobs we do most here

Shower pumps

Period houses can need the correct pump type for an upstairs shower.

Cold water tanks

Older loft storage needs sound valves, lids, supports and insulation.

Toilet repairs

Mansion and purpose-built flats often need ordinary cistern repair rather than full replacement.

Tap repairs and replacement

Pressure suitability matters when fitting new mixers in older systems.

Plumbing inspections

Buyers can check pressure, supply materials, tanks and cylinders before exchange.

Access and parking

Putney uses different visitor-parking arrangements from Fulham, so the resident or client needs to tell us what applies at the property. We do not assume the same permit process carries across the bridge.

The High Street and Putney Bridge can become congested at peak periods. We agree an arrival window that allows for traffic rather than promising a journey time that cannot be guaranteed.

Local questions

Do you really cover Putney from Fulham?

Yes. The focus is the part of Putney closest to the bridge and mainly north of the Upper Richmond Road.

How does parking work for you in Putney?

It depends on the street and building. Tell us whether there is a visitor arrangement or paid bay and we will plan around that.

Can you inspect the plumbing before we buy in Putney?

Yes. A plumbing inspection can check the stopcock, pressure, visible pipe materials, tanks, cylinders and common fittings.

Do you work in mansion blocks?

Yes. Access, shared services and any porter or lift arrangements are agreed before the visit.
