How we work

Send a photo first

A clear photo and postcode often help identify the likely parts and whether the visit looks like a one-hour job or needs more time.

Clear price structure

Labour starts at £75 for the first hour. Further time is charged in 30-minute steps once the real increment is configured. Parts are charged at cost.

Explain before fixing

Where there is more than one sensible route, we explain what has failed and what each repair involves before continuing.

Tidy and itemised

The aim is a sound repair, a clean work area and an invoice that separates labour from parts.

What we do

Leaks, toilets, taps, showers, pumps, water pressure, tanks, cylinders, stopcocks, radiator valves, internal wastes, appliance connections, outside taps and small plumbing jobs.

What we don't do

We don't work on boilers or gas appliances, we don't do drain jetting, and we don't fit new bathrooms. For a complete new bathroom or en-suite, see En-Suites & Bathrooms.
