Questions

What people ask first.

How much does a plumber cost in Fulham?

£75 for the first hour, then charged in 30-minute steps, with parts at cost. There is no separate call-out fee.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Small repairs are the core of the service: taps, toilets, valves, wastes, pumps and lists of smaller jobs.

Can you come today?

Availability varies. Use the contact page with the problem and postcode and we will tell you the earliest realistic time once contact details are live.

Do you bring parts?

A clear photo helps identify the fitting and improves the chance of bringing the right parts on the first visit.

Which areas do you cover?

Fulham SW6 is the core area, with selected nearby work in Chelsea Harbour and Lots Road, Putney and Wandsworth Town.

Do you work for landlords and agents?

Yes. Access can be arranged with tenants, with photos and clear invoicing for the landlord or agent. See our landlord and agent plumbing page for details.

What we don't do

We don't work on boilers or gas appliances, we don't do drain jetting, and we don't fit new bathrooms. For a complete new bathroom or en-suite, see En-Suites & Bathrooms.
