Included

What the labour covers.

Diagnosis and plumbing labour within the booked visit, with parts shown separately at cost.

Separate unless agreed

What isn't included.

Making good, decorating and electrical work are separate unless specifically quoted. We explain where another trade is needed.

Questions

Pricing questions.

What's included in the first hour?

Labour from arrival for up to the first hour. Parts are separate at cost and shown on the invoice.

Is there a call-out fee?

No. There is no separate call-out fee; the first-hour labour charge is the starting charge for a visit.

How do you charge for parts?

Parts are charged at cost and shown separately on the invoice.

Do you give fixed quotes?

For bigger jobs we can, after seeing it.

What we don't do

We don't work on boilers or gas appliances, we don't do drain jetting, and we don't fit new bathrooms. For a complete new bathroom or en-suite, see En-Suites & Bathrooms.
