Questions

Common questions.

Can you deal with tenants directly?

Yes. With the landlord or agent’s authority, access can be arranged directly with the tenant so the person at the property gets the appointment details first hand.

Do you invoice the agent?

The invoice can be addressed to the agreed party. Any account terms are confirmed before work starts rather than assumed on the website.

Can you send photos before and after?

Yes. Where useful, photos can show the visible fault, the part that failed and the finished plumbing repair.

Can you do a list of repairs between tenancies?

Yes. Send the list with photos and we can group the plumbing items into one planned visit where practical.

Have a repair list?

Send the postcode, access contact and photos.
